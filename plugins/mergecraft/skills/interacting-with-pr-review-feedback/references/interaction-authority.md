# Interaction Authority

Use one ordinary forge operation for the requested leaf interaction only after
current state and authority are established. Reply authority does not imply
reaction or resolution authority. Re-read the result by stable identity and
verify the operation, actor, and exact body or reaction. For a reply or reaction,
verify that the thread's resolution state is unchanged. Only a requested
resolution operation may verify a transition to resolved.

Do not create top-level PR comments or change title, body, draft/ready state,
labels, reviews, checks, source, or merge state from this capability.
