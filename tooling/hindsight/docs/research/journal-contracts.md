## Scope and finding

This inventory covers only `tooling/hindsight` source, documentation, and tests at repaired-source revision `7b165b3ee97e7e4bbddcb8eafe089f7f1237e0ab`. **Conflict with the fixed publication requirement.** The current stopped-run writer durably publishes and directory-syncs a two-field, unauthenticated pending marker before expiry, samples `durable_at`, builds the digest-bound journal with that sample, atomically replaces the marker, and directory-syncs again. It does not sample or check the clock after the authenticated replacement or its directory sync. The source therefore establishes a durable *pending start* before expiry, not genuine durable authenticated publication before expiry, and `applied_at` or `recorded_at` records the earlier marker boundary rather than actual authenticated publication. (`tooling/hindsight/bin/hindsight-memory` lines 839–1013 at `7b165b3e`) A source test intentionally demonstrates the mismatch: it advances the clock to expiry during the authenticated replacement, accepts the write after the second directory sync, and retains the earlier `applied_at`. That is test evidence of current behavior, not a durability or deployment proof. (`tooling/hindsight/tests/test_hindsight_memory_operation_recovery_cli.py` lines 450–503 at `7b165b3e`)

No product code or tests were executed for this research.

Later work remains constrained, but not designed here, by genuine durable authenticated publication before the nonrenewing approval expires; a timestamp for that actual publication rather than an earlier boundary; separate apply and rollback approvals; exact historical evidence and replay bindings; no retry, cohort, or budget expansion; and an explicit human decision before introducing any new storage component.

## Actual stopped application path

1. **Preparation is non-authorizing.** `operation-recovery drain stopped-run plan` requires the reference plan, authorization, exact application journal, frozen progress, grant ledger, process and provider quiescence, terminal absence, a complete live snapshot, rollback-backup evidence and attestation, and all output paths. The constructor accepts only stopped references at schema 15 or 16, requires an expired open grant claim without a close, binds the exact evidence and row lineage, and emits `authority: unapproved-plan` with `mutation_authorized: false`. Evidence may be at most 300 seconds old; the plan lasts 3,600 seconds; the database transaction timeout is 120 seconds. (`tooling/hindsight/bin/hindsight-memory` lines 19453–19539 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 15333–15606 at `7b165b3e`)
2. **Apply approval and rollback preparation precede the journal.** Apply requires the exact plan digest as approval, rechecks the plan, candidate, installation authority, grant ledger, rollback-backup digest, and continued absence of historical terminal status, then creates an authorization receipt and encrypted selected-row preimage bundle. The decrypted preimage is verified against the plan before journal creation. (`tooling/hindsight/bin/hindsight-memory` lines 15137–15355 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 17108–17238 at `7b165b3e`)
3. **Publication is the current conflicting boundary.** The application journal is a closed value binding the plan, authorization, rollback bundle, historical plan and journal, frozen progress, grant ledger, pre/post generations, selected and preserved row-set digests, `applied_at`, and its own digest. The pending marker is replaced with this value as described above. The deterministic postimage derives its timestamps and digest from the journal's `applied_at`. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 1970–1988 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 17239–17279 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 15858–15931 at `7b165b3e`)
4. **Initial mutation is an exact, idempotent database CAS.** Only selected rows proved to remain `processing` are changed. A prior retry count below three becomes `pending`, increments the retry count, clears the active claim, and preserves payload, result metadata, error evidence, and checkpoint lineage; a prior count of three becomes `failed`, stays at three, and preserves the historical owner. All other cohort rows remain digest-identical. The serializable transaction locks the generation and cohort, requires exclusive database access and queue guards, advances generation exactly once, and recognizes the exact postimage as already applied. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 15113–15330 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery_runtime.py` lines 9043–9314 at `7b165b3e`)
5. **Interrupted continuation requires the authenticated application journal.** Once it exists, apply may continue after plan expiry. The executor verifies every existing closed artifact, recreates only missing successors, re-applies the exact database operation, and requires the second application call to report `already_applied`. It then completes interrupted, stopped, application, and verification receipts. These receipts explicitly report a stopped reconciliation, never historical drain success. Neither an authorization receipt nor a pending marker alone satisfies the post-expiry continuation gate. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 17131–17436 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/bin/hindsight-memory` lines 15262–15309 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/bin/hindsight-memory` lines 15575–15676 at `7b165b3e`)
6. **Verification feeds bounded descendants.** The complete-chain verifier reconstructs the journal-derived postimage and all receipt values. Handoff construction adds a fresh cleanup snapshot, selected checkpoints, prior retry lineage, attempt ledger, preserved-row digests, and a 3,600-second evidence-only lifetime; it does not authorize mutation. Schema-17 exact-drain planning and schema-14 post-abort planning revalidate the source chain through that handoff. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 17439–17591 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 10404–10921 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 6075–6107 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 13551–13602 at `7b165b3e`)

## Distinct rollback path

1. Rollback approval is a different digest over the action, plan, completed application and verification receipts, postimage, full preimage and row-set digests, original grant identity, grant-use record, and grant ledger. Supplying the apply approval cannot authorize rollback. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 17594–17670 at `7b165b3e`)
2. Rollback first verifies the entire forward chain, creates a distinct rollback authorization receipt, then publishes the separate rollback journal through the same conflicting pending-marker writer. The rollback journal binds the completed forward receipts, exact postimage and preimage, current and rollback generations, selected and preserved row sets, grant ledger, `recorded_at`, and its own digest. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 2086–2105 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 17853–17979 at `7b165b3e`)
3. The CLI decrypts the already-bound selected-row preimage only when the rollback callback first runs. The serializable transaction restores only selected rows, requires the exact postimage and queue guards, advances generation once again rather than rewinding it, and treats the exact rollback generation plus preimage as already applied. It never restores grant authority or mutates the grant ledger. (`tooling/hindsight/bin/hindsight-memory` lines 15360–15569 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery_runtime.py` lines 9317–9584 at `7b165b3e`)
4. A valid rollback journal permits exact continuation after expiry. The executor invokes rollback again and requires `already_applied`, then creates the rollback receipt; the public chain verifier authenticates the forward and rollback families together. A pending marker does not pass that verifier. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 17981–18133 at `7b165b3e`)

## Compatibility constraints established by source

- **Historical evidence keeps its meaning.** The exact-drain reference journal's `started_at == authorized_at` rule, worker identity and attempt, grant identity, start-gate digest, and progress binding are inputs to stopped planning. A new publication timestamp cannot silently redefine `started_at` or break old schema-2 journal verification. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery_progress.py` lines 1177–1247 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 15367–15440 at `7b165b3e`)
- **Stopped schemas remain reference-dependent.** Reference schema 16 maps to stopped schema 1; reference schema 15 maps to stopped schema 2. Other reference schemas are rejected. Readers require closed, exact key sets. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 14387–14419 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 1970–2105 at `7b165b3e`)
- **No retry, cohort, or budget expansion.** Reconciliation settles only interrupted rows already selected by the reference plan and consumes exactly one ordinary attempt. Handoff descendants remain a subset of the reference-selected rows, preserve every other row digest, retain one reconciliation-cycle ceiling, and enforce a per-operation cumulative ceiling of `ordinary_attempt_ceiling * 5`; prior retry lineage is part of the calculation. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 15243–15329 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 10253–10397 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 10483–10573 at `7b165b3e`)
- **Apply, rollback, stopped outcome, and historical success remain distinct.** Separate approval and journal paths are mandatory; stopped application and verification receipts set `successful_drain: false`; rollback leaves the historical grant and ledger unchanged. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 2007–2120 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 18047–18056 at `7b165b3e`)
- **Sibling continuation contracts are not interchangeable.** Grant retirement starts at its authorization receipt; claim-release and shared requeue/post-abort journals use ordinary create-and-overwrite lifecycles. Changing those behaviors is an explicit scope and compatibility decision, not an incidental consequence of repairing stopped publication. (`tooling/hindsight/lib/hindsight_memory_control_plane/operation_recovery.py` lines 10961–11147 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/bin/hindsight-memory` lines 18258–18353 at `7b165b3e`)

## Test-only evidence and proof gaps

**Test-only evidence.** Static inspection found tests that model expiry at the writer boundary, reject the pending marker as a journal, exercise apply crashes after every artifact and database boundary, require a distinct rollback approval, replay rollback after each boundary, and exercise exact apply/rollback transactions against PostgreSQL fixtures. The contract crash tests use an in-memory durable writer that samples twice and then places the final value directly in a dictionary; it does not model the final filesystem replacement or directory sync. (`tooling/hindsight/tests/test_hindsight_memory_operation_recovery_cli.py` lines 260–503 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/tests/test_hindsight_memory_operation_recovery.py` lines 81–114 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/tests/test_hindsight_memory_operation_recovery.py` lines 12434–12585 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/tests/test_hindsight_memory_operation_recovery.py` lines 12765–13024 at `7b165b3e`, `tooling/hindsight/tests/test_hindsight_memory_operation_recovery_postgres.py` lines 2994–3665 at `7b165b3e`)

**Unknowns and proof gaps.**

- Source does not provide a post-authenticated-sync timestamp or deadline check, so it cannot prove the required publication time or prevent substitution of the earlier marker time.
- Source has no continuation or recovery transition for an interrupted pending marker. Its deployed population and any operational handling are unknown because runtime and incident artifacts were outside scope.
- The inspected tests do not prove power-loss behavior, deployment filesystem behavior, or atomic recovery across the file publication and database transaction. A passing source test would not establish durability or deployment behavior.
- The CLI requires `--plan` and follows plan-bound artifact paths. This source boundary establishes no deployment-independent journal catalog or restart discovery mechanism. Whether to add a storage component is therefore an unresolved, explicit architecture decision, not an established requirement. (`tooling/hindsight/bin/hindsight-memory` lines 19542–19599 at `7b165b3e`)
- No claim is made about revisions after `7b165b3ee97e7e4bbddcb8eafe089f7f1237e0ab`, production state, or the contents of the independent assessment at `e30ee145efab8aeb766a6b2bec02c5e0c3f6a82e`.

## Inputs required for later human decisions

1. **Publication boundary and time semantics:** define which authenticated bytes must be durable before expiry and which field records that actual boundary, while preserving the historical exact-drain `started_at` contract and deciding how existing stopped `applied_at` and `recorded_at` values are read.
2. **On-disk compatibility:** decide the schema/version and reader behavior for existing stopped schemas 1 and 2, the two-field pending marker, partially completed artifact prefixes, and complete historical chains.
3. **Restart authority:** decide the authenticated evidence that permits post-expiry continuation for apply and separately for rollback, including the treatment of a pending marker that is present but unverifiable.
4. **Scope:** decide whether publication changes are confined to the two stopped journals or explicitly include grant retirement, claim release, or shared requeue/post-abort journals. Preserve each untouched sibling's current start and overwrite semantics.
5. **Storage ownership:** decide explicitly whether plan-bound private files remain the sole persistence/discovery mechanism or whether a new storage component is in scope; no such component is adopted by this inventory.
6. **Acceptance evidence:** identify the target filesystem and deployment boundary, required crash and restart points, clock-boundary cases, and database/file cross-boundary checks needed to prove genuine durable authenticated publication and exact replay. Keep retry, cohort, and budget ceilings fixed.
